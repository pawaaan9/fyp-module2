  A trained model cannot beat the rule it is imitating, so compare each
  model against its rule ceiling rather than against 100%.

  If pos RECOVERED and neg INVERTED on the same probes, Module 2 transmits
  the scorer's direction faithfully in both directions -- it reports what the
  scorer says rather than always reporting improvement. Step 2's result for
  the real GAT is then a fact about that model, not a pipeline artefact.

  REMINDER: the synthetic models are geometry controls, not football models.
